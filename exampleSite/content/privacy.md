This site has no cookies, no tracking scripts and no comments. The server keeps standard access logs for 14 days to keep it running, and then deletes them.

## The newsletter

If you sign up for the letter, your email address and first name are stored by the mailing service I use, only to send you the letter. I can see whether an issue was opened, in total, not per reader. Every letter has a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes your address.

## Questions

Write to [hello@platformnotes.example](mailto:hello@platformnotes.example) and I will tell you exactly what is stored about you, which for almost everyone is nothing.
