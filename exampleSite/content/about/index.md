I am Adaeze Morgan, a software engineer in Leeds. In 2019 I took a sleeper from London to Vienna for a conference, slept better than I had on any flight, and have not been on a plane since. That was about 60,000 km ago.

Platform Notes is where I write down what I learn on the way: which seats to book, what a berth really costs, the stations worth a missed connection. I build timetable software for a living, so now and then a post is about the small scripts I write to plan trips.

## How I write

Every route on this site is one I have taken myself, and every price is one I paid, with the month I paid it. When a fare or a timetable changes, I update the post and say so at the top. Nobody pays to be mentioned here, and I do not use affiliate links.

## Get in touch

Corrections are the email I like best: [hello@platformnotes.example](mailto:hello@platformnotes.example). I read everything and answer most of it, usually from a train.

## The letter

Once a month, on the first Sunday, I send the posts you might have missed and the sleeper fares that dropped that month. About 3,400 people read it.
